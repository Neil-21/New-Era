# Anthropic

Anthropic develops the Claude family of AI products and models. This page is a
neutral research note for considering how AI can support retail work such as
catalogue analysis, product copy, customer support, and internal operations.

## Research checklist

- [ ] Separate general retail use cases from any announced consumer shopping feature.
- [ ] Verify product availability and policy details against Anthropic's own documentation.
- [ ] Capture constraints, approval steps, and evaluation criteria before any purchase flow.

Anthropic's retail material describes operational and e-commerce use cases; it
is not treated here as evidence of an in-chat checkout product.
