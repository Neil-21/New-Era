# OpenAI

OpenAI develops AI products and models used through ChatGPT and developer
tools. For this demo vault, this page is a place to track product notes and
source links rather than a complete company profile.

## Shopping notes

ChatGPT can present product results for shopping questions. OpenAI says these
results are selected on relevance rather than paid placement; eligible products
and merchants may offer Instant Checkout in ChatGPT.

- [ ] Check regional and merchant availability before relying on checkout.
- [ ] Record the source and date for any product claim.

Sources: [Shopping with ChatGPT Search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-improved-shopping-results-from-chatgpt-search), [Instant Checkout and the Agentic Commerce Protocol](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/).
