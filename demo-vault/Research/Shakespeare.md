# Shakespeare

William Shakespeare was an English playwright, poet, and actor. His work spans
comedies, histories, tragedies, and late romances, and remains central to the
study and performance of English-language theatre.

## Starting points

- *Hamlet* — memory, grief, action, and delay.
- *Macbeth* — ambition, power, and moral consequence.
- *A Midsummer Night's Dream* — love, performance, and mischief.
